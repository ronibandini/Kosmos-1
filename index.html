Critical and minicritical days

Red / Critical days

Yellow / Minicritical

About these interpretations

05 · Conversion method

Why the dates can be shifted

The three Kosmos cycles are 23, 28 and 33 days. Their common period is 21,252 days (23 × 28 × 33).

The converter subtracts complete 21,252-day periods from both dates until both dates fit the Kosmos-1's two-digit-year range of 1901–1999.

Both dates are shifted by exactly the same number of days, so their elapsed interval is unchanged. Since 21,252 is also divisible by 7, the weekday is preserved.
