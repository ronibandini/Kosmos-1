How it works

Traditional biorhythm theory proposes three repeating cycles that begin at birth. Each cycle is represented by a sine wave.

Physical — 23 days

Traditionally associated with stamina and physical energy.

Emotional — 28 days

Traditionally associated with mood and emotional state.

Intellectual — 33 days

Traditionally associated with concentration and mental performance.

These are historical concepts, not scientifically validated measures of physical, emotional, or intellectual performance.
