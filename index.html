How it works

P: physical S: sensitivity I: intellectual.
Red/Critical days: body or mind is less stable on these days, making you more prone to accidents, emotional missteps, or poor concentration in that specific domain (physical, emotional, or intellectual)
Yellow/Minicritical: represents a state of depleted reserve—such as severe physical fatigue (-100% physical), deep sadness or burnout (-100% emotional), or mental exhaustion and brain fog (-100% intellectual)

The three Kosmos cycles are 23, 28 and 33 days. Their common period is 21,252 days (23 × 28 × 33). The converter subtracts complete 21,252-day periods from both dates until both dates fit the Kosmos-1's two-digit-year range of 1901–1999.

Both dates are shifted by exactly the same number of days, so their elapsed interval is unchanged. Since 21,252 is also divisible by 7, the weekday is preserved.
